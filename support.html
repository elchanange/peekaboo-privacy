PrivacySupport

Peekaboo takes photos of your baby looking right at you. Tap the yellow button: a friend pops up with a sound, your baby looks, and Peekaboo takes three photos and marks the best one as a favorite in Photos.

Common questions

There is no sound.
Turn up the volume. Peekaboo plays sounds even with the silent switch on. Check that Sound in Settings is not set to No sound.
Where are my photos?
In the Photos app. The best photo of each peekaboo is in Favorites.
The camera is black.
Allow the camera for Peekaboo in the iPhone Settings app, under Privacy & Security, Camera.
Can my baby watch the screen instead?
Yes. Tap the turn around button. The friend appears beside the front camera, and you take photos by tapping anywhere or pressing a volume button.
How do I record my own voice?
Open Settings in the app (the gear), then Your voice. Babies turn toward a voice they know.
On iPhone Duo
Open the phone and use the back camera. You see the camera on the big screen, and your baby sees the friend on the outside screen, right beside the lens.
